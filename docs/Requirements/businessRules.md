# Business Rules (BR)

* **BR01**: Name must be at least 3 characters.
* **BR02**: Cpf must be valid.
* **BR03**: Cpf must be at least 4 characters.
* **BR04**: Account can be deleted by providing the correct current password.
* **BR05**: Expense amount must be at least 1.
* **BR06**: Expense date must be in the past.
